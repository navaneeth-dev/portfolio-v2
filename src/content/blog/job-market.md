Paradox:

- Getting the job is harder than learning the skills for the job.
- Job hunting is a job itself.

So let's dive into why you can't get interviews first.

## 1. Supply-Demand Issue

Each job nowadays gets 100 applications within a day and 300-2000 in two weeks, this means interviewing every single
candidate is not possible. So what is the solution? That's right AI aka ATS. The ATS assigns a score to each candidate
and sorts them. Recruiters then see the top 10-20, interview 10 and finally select 1-2 people.

*THIS* is the reason you are not getting interviews, YOU ARE NOT AT THE TOP OF THE LIST.

What is the solution? There isn't! Both sides are suffering. 90% of candidates are not skilled but the skilled ones are
getting lost in the ATS.

## 2. COVID

When lockdown ended, IT market normalized from its peak. This means many companies had to scale down, spending in tech
reduced, people were fired.

## 3. Senior Market

We are going through a senior favored market right now. Why? Because of the previous point. When there are loads of
seniors willing to work for less pay, why hire a junior? So instead of hiring 2 juniors we can hire 2 seniors.
